      How to get your cookies.txt

      3 steps — export cookies from your browser while logged into YouTube.

      1. 1
        Open YouTube & log in

        Use the same browser you normally watch YouTube with, while you're signed in.

      2. 2
        Open DevTools console

        Press F12 → Console tab (or Ctrl+Shift+J).

      3. 3
        Paste this snippet & press Enter

        It copies your cookies as cookies.txt to your clipboard.

        copy([...document.cookie.split('; ').map(c => {
  const [n, ...v] = c.split('=');
  return ['.youtube.com','TRUE','/','FALSE','0', n, v.join('=')].join('\t');
})].join('\n'))

      Tip: paste the copied text into the box, then hit Save cookies. HttpOnly login cookies may require the "Get cookies.txt" browser extension instead.
